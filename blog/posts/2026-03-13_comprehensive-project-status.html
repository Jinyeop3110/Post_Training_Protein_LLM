Comprehensive Project Status: SFT to GRPO, What We Know After 25 Experiments

Date: 2026-03-13 | Tags: sft, grpo, rl, training, evaluation, milestone | Experiments: 4 SFT + 13 GRPO runs

Executive Summary

After 25 experiments spanning SFT and GRPO phases on Qwen3-8B, the ESM3+MLP approach achieves eval_loss 0.361 on SFT (70.1% better than text-only at 1.207). GRPO on the structure quality task shows the strongest learning signal: text-only GRPO reaches mean_reward 0.832 while MLP plateaus at 0.582, revealing a critical gradient routing problem where MLP's multimodal and LoRA pathways cannot both update simultaneously. Frozen-MM experiments partially resolve this, reaching 0.774 reward with LoRA-only updates. Classification-based reward reformulation is under active investigation.

Finding 4: The Gradient Routing Problem. In MLP GRPO, gradients flow exclusively to either LoRA or multimodal parameters, never both simultaneously. In ProteinLM bench, only LoRA updates (MM grad=0). In structure quality, only the projector updates (LoRA grad=0). The text-only approach avoids this by having a single gradient pathway (LoRA only), reaching reward 0.832 vs MLP's 0.582.
Finding 5: Freezing multimodal params partially resolves routing. The frozen-MM experiment forces all gradients through LoRA, achieving reward 0.774 vs 0.582 for unfrozen MLP. However, text-only still outperforms (0.832) because it benefits from direct token-level interaction with protein sequences during RL generation.

4. Open Questions and Anomalies

  • Gradient routing: Why can MLP not update both LoRA and projector simultaneously during GRPO? This is the most critical open question.
  • pLDDT calibration: Frozen-MM predicts pLDDT=85.1 vs actual 79.7 (overestimation); unfrozen MLP predicts 67.9 (underestimation). Text-only is well-calibrated at 79.1.
  • Classification reformulation: Text classify reaches 0.710 with 58.2% zero-std, suggesting the classification framing may be too easy (majority of groups agree). MLP classify at 0.598 with 17.7% zero-std has more variance but lower reward.
  • Perceiver pathway: Still untested at 4.89M scale. Only has empty log directory from 0308 attempt.

5. Recommendations

  1. For SFT deployment: Use MLP checkpoint-9750 (eval_loss 0.361). Text checkpoint-8000 (eval_loss 1.207) is the best text baseline.
  2. For GRPO: Text-only is currently the better RL pathway. If MLP GRPO is essential, freeze multimodal params and use LoRA-only updates.
  3. Debug gradient routing: Investigate the mutual exclusion of LoRA and multimodal gradients in GRPO. This may be a framework issue (FSDP interaction, loss routing) rather than a fundamental limitation.
  4. Retry Perceiver SFT: The perceiver pathway failed to launch and should be retried for the four-way comparison thesis.
  5. Consider two-stage GRPO: First stage with frozen-MM to teach format compliance via LoRA, second stage with unfrozen-MM for reward-specific learning. This could combine the strengths of both gradient pathways.
